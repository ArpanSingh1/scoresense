Type exactly what the student wrote. Nothing you type leaves this computer.
How to label
  • Copy the answer exactly: keep spelling mistakes, capitals, spaces and punctuation as written (photosynthesys, x=7, 42,5).
  • Leave out anything the student crossed out.
  • Nothing written: press Blank answer.
  • Can't read it: press Can't read.
  • Not sure yet: press Skip; skipped crops stay unlabelled and are left out of the score.
  • Enter saves and moves on. Download labels.csv when finished.
